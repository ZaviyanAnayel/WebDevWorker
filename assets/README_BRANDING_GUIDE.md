# WebDevWorker — Brand Identity & Design System

Yeh brand kit **WebDevWorker.com** ke liye tayar ki gayi hai jo luxury gold aur platinum ribbon aesthetic par mabni hai.

## 🎨 Official Brand Palette
- **Primary Gold:** `#EAB308` (Metallic Accent & Highlights)
- **Deep Gold / Shadow:** `#CA8A04` & `#A16207`
- **Platinum Silver:** `#F1F5F9` to `#94A3B8`
- **Dark Void Background:** `#020617` / `#0F172A`
- **Pure White Text:** `#FFFFFF`

## 📁 Included Vector Files:
1. `webdevworker-logo-horizontal.svg`: Website navbar aur header ke liye (transparent background).
2. `webdevworker-logo-vertical.svg`: Hero banner, splash screen, aur social cards ke liye.
3. `webdevworker-icon.svg`: 512×512 App Icon aur Favicon (PWA installable app ke liye).
