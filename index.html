Questions

Is my workflow uploaded anywhere?

No. The audit runs 100% in your browser. Your workflow JSON never leaves your machine — there is no server, no account, and no upload.

Which tools does it check?

n8n, Make and Zapier workflow exports. Anything else is scanned as raw text for hardcoded secrets and private-network URLs.

Do I need an API key?

No. The core audit is deterministic and needs no key at all — that is the point. Your workflow is checked by rules, not by sending it to an AI.

What does the score mean?

90–100: safe to activate. 60–89: fix the high-severity findings first. 0–59: do not activate yet — a critical risk is open.
